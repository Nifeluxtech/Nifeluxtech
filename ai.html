About Our AI Division

Making Intelligence Practical

The Nifelux AI division focuses on creating artificial intelligence that works—real systems solving real problems in African contexts. We move beyond demonstrations to build AI that integrates into daily life, business operations, and critical services.

Our research spans machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and agentic AI systems. We develop platforms that learn from African data, operate in African languages, and address African challenges.
